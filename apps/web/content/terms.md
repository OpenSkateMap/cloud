# Terms of use

*Last updated 15 October 2026. Questions: {{contact}}.*

## Who can use OpenSkateMap

Anyone at least 13, or the minimum age where you live if that's higher, with one account per person, signed in with their own Apple Account.

## What you add

- **Real spots people can skate.** Curators decline spots on private property without permission (backyard pools, private parks), spots you can only reach by trespassing, and spots locals want kept off the map.
- **Accurate details.** Put the pin where the spot is, and describe it honestly.
- **Photos you have the right to share.** Credit whoever took the photo. If it wasn't you, you need their permission to share it here; the app asks you to confirm that.

## Your content and our licence

You keep the rights to the photos and words you add. By submitting them, you give OpenSkateMap a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to store, copy, resize, crop, re-encode, display and distribute them in OpenSkateMap, with the credit you entered, and to show a spot's name and cover photo in the preview of a link to it. Curators may edit a submission before it goes on the map (move the pin, fix the title or the obstacles, choose the photos). Because the map is built together, the licence continues after you delete your account: the spots and photos you contributed stay on the map without your name.

You also allow OpenSkateMap to publish the facts about the spots you add (name, location, kind, condition and obstacles), never your photos or descriptions, under an open data licence that lets anyone reuse them with credit to OpenSkateMap.

## House rules

Content that breaks these is removed, and accounts that break them are disabled.

- **No objectionable content**: nothing sexual, violent or gory, hateful or discriminatory, harassing or threatening, and nothing illegal or encouraging crime.
- **Respect people**: no private information about anyone (home addresses, phone numbers), and no photos posted to embarrass or expose someone.
- **No spam or ads**: no selling, advertising or promoting other services, and no fake or duplicate submissions.
- **Be yourself**: no impersonating other people or brands.
- **No abuse of the service**: no scripts or automation that copy the map or flood it with submissions.

## Curators

A curator checks every spot and photo before it appears on the map. Curators are local skaters trusted with their city; admins appoint them. They approve, edit or decline submissions, and you see the reason when one is declined. We may remove any spot, photo or profile that breaks these terms or turns out to be wrong.

## Reports and blocks

Report a spot or a person from the app, and block anyone whose profile you can see. Someone on our team reviews every report, and we act on content that breaks these terms as soon as we can. We don't share what happened with a report, to protect everyone's privacy.

## Skate at your own risk

Skateboarding is dangerous. Spot details come from the community and can be wrong or out of date: spots get knobbed, fenced off, demolished or patrolled. Check a spot yourself, respect the people around you, the property and the local rules, and wear protection. OpenSkateMap doesn't encourage trespassing or breaking any law, and you're responsible for where and how you skate.

## Our side

OpenSkateMap is provided as it is, without promises that a spot is safe, legal to skate, accurate or still there, or that the app is always available. As far as the law allows, we're not liable for injuries, damage or fines that come from skating a spot you found here.

## Ending

You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete Account. We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms.

## Changes

We'll tell you in the app before material changes apply.
