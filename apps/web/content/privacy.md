# Privacy policy

*Last updated 15 November 2026. Questions: {{contact}}.*

## What OpenSkateMap is

OpenSkateMap is a map of skate spots, curated by local skaters. You sign in with Apple, find spots on the map and add the ones you know. A curator checks every submission before it goes on the map.

## What we collect, and why

- **Your Sign in with Apple details**: the identifier Apple gives us for your account, your email address (often a private relay address that Apple forwards to you), and your name if you choose to share it. *Why:* to create your account and sign you in. We never see your Apple Account password.
- **Your profile**: display name, @username, bio, social handles (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Telegram, website) and avatar photo. *Why:* so other skaters can see who added a spot and find you. You can change it any time in Edit Profile.
- **The spots you submit**: the title, kind, condition, obstacles and description, the spot's position on the map, and a note to the curator. *Why:* so a curator can review the spot and, once it's approved, show it on the map. Your note is only for curators and is never shown on the spot.
- **The photos you add**, and any short clip that plays in place of one: the image or clip, who took it (the credit you enter), whether you have the photographer's permission, and a link to where you found it, if you give one. Your phone removes the photo's location and camera details before upload, and our server re-encodes every photo so only the image is left; of a clip it keeps only the silent video. *Why:* to show the spot. Photos and clips you upload but never use (in a submission, as your avatar or as a collection's picture) are deleted after a week.
- **Your collections**: their names and descriptions, the spots in them, whether each one is public or private, the people you share them with, and the email addresses you invite (kept until that person opens the app or you cancel the invite). A spot you add to one of your private collections isn't reviewed by a curator and never goes on the map. *Why:* to keep your collections and share them with the people you choose. How many people keep a spot in a collection also helps rank that spot; nobody sees who.
- **Your votes on spots**: whether you voted each spot up or down. *Why:* to show how skaters rate each spot, and to recommend spots. Others see only the totals, never who voted.
- **Curators' decisions**: who approved, edited or declined each submission, when, and the reason they gave, and the reason an admin gave for taking a spot down. *Why:* to show you what happened to your submissions and to keep a record of every change to the map. Only admins can see which curator made a decision.
- **Reports and blocks**: what you reported (a spot or a person), the reason and what you wrote, and who you blocked. *Why:* so a person on our team can review every report, and so people you blocked stay away from your profile.
- **Sign-in sessions**: for each device you're signed in on, its IP address, the app and device version it reports, and when it started. *Why:* to keep you signed in and to stop abuse. A session ends when you sign out, or after 60 days without use.
- **Apple's token for your account**, stored encrypted. *Why:* only so we can ask Apple to revoke OpenSkateMap's access when you delete your account.
- **Notifications**, if you allow them: your device's push token, the app version, device model, language and time zone, the area your connection comes from (country, region and city), the city your map was last in, which kinds of notifications you turned off, and when you open one. *Why:* to send the notifications you chose: curators' decisions on what you added, new spots to review if you're a curator, collections shared with you, and new spots near you. You can turn each kind off in Profile → Notifications.
- **Tips**, if you leave one: Apple handles the payment, and RevenueCat receives the purchase with a random identifier that isn't linked to your account. *Why:* to confirm the tip. A tip unlocks nothing.
- **Usage analytics**, unless you turn them off in Profile → Share Analytics: which screens you open and when the app opens and closes; how you opened a spot (from the map, the list, search or a link); when you get directions to a spot, share it or filter the map; and that you submitted, voted on, reported or reviewed something (not what you wrote), with your account's identifier, the app version, your device model, language and the area your connection comes from (country, region and city), but not your IP address. Never your name, email or anything you write. *Why:* to see which parts of OpenSkateMap skaters use and fix what doesn't work. PostHog stores it for us.
- **Visits to openskatemap.app**: which pages are opened, the links clicked, the browser, device type and the area the connection comes from (country, region and city). No cookies and nothing stored in your browser; visitors are counted with a daily hash PostHog can't turn back into you. *Why:* to see how people find OpenSkateMap.
- **Your location** stays on your phone. The app reads it only when you tap the location button on the map or choose Use My Location for a new spot. We receive a location only as the position of a spot you add, or of a pin you check for spots already nearby.

We don't use advertising or track you across other apps and websites, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it to train AI models.

## Who sees what

- **Everyone signed in to OpenSkateMap** sees approved spots and their photos with the credit you entered, and how many skaters voted each spot up or down; your public profile (name, @username, avatar, bio, handles, when you joined, how many spots you submitted and got approved, your cities, and your role if you're a curator); and your name on the spots you added.
- **Anyone with a link to a spot**, and the apps that preview links (Messages, WhatsApp, Telegram, X and others), see that spot's name, kind, city and cover photo on openskatemap.app, without the photo's credit. Not where the spot is, its description or who added it. These pages ask search engines not to list them.
- **People in a collection with you** see it, its spots (including private spots added to it), your name and who else is in it. Its owner and admins also see who's invited (the @username, or the email address typed) and who asked to join.
- **Anyone signed in with the link to a public collection** sees it, its spots and your name, and your public collections are listed on your profile.
- **Curators and admins** also see pending submissions, your notes to them and your answers about photo permission. Admins see reports.
- **Whoever added a spot** sees the edits and videos you suggest for it, with your note and photo answers, so they can approve or decline them first.
- **Only you** see your email address.
- **People you block** can't see your profile, and their names don't show on spots you open.

## Service providers

They process data only to run OpenSkateMap:

- **Cloudflare** runs our servers and stores photos.
- **A managed Postgres database** stores accounts, profiles, spots and submissions.
- **Apple** provides Sign in with Apple, delivers notifications and takes payment for tips.
- **BuzzKit**, an open-source notification service, sends notifications. We run it ourselves, on the same Cloudflare and database accounts.
- **RevenueCat** records tips.
- **PostHog** stores usage analytics, if you leave them on, and website visits, in the United States.

## How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. If you stop using Sign in with Apple with OpenSkateMap, Apple tells us and we sign you out on every device. If you delete your Apple Account, we delete your OpenSkateMap account 30 days later, as Delete Account does, unless you sign in again before then. Sessions end as described above. Request logs (the address asked for, the response code and timing, your IP address) and error logs (error names and codes, never what you wrote) are kept by our hosting provider for at most 7 days, to find and fix problems and to stop abuse.

## Your choices

- **Edit** your profile any time in Profile → Edit Profile.
- **Delete** your account in Profile → Delete Account. It deletes your account right away, with your profile, avatar, sessions, blocks, votes, notification settings and push tokens, your usage analytics, and the photos and clips you never submitted, and asks Apple to revoke OpenSkateMap's access. Spots and photos you contributed, and reports you made, stay without your name. It can't be undone.
- **Turn off usage analytics** in Profile → Share Analytics. From then on nothing is sent about you, by the app or by our servers.
- **Turn notifications off** by kind in Profile → Notifications, or all of them in Settings → OpenSkateMap → Notifications on your iPhone.
- **Stop sharing your location** in Settings → OpenSkateMap → Location on your iPhone.

You can also write to {{contact}} to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it.

## Children

OpenSkateMap is not for children under 13, or under the minimum age where they live if that's higher.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.
