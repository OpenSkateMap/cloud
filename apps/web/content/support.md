# Support

*Questions, problems or ideas: write to {{contact}}. A person reads every message.*

## Write to us from the app

Profile → Send Feedback opens an email to us with your app version and device already filled in, and attaches the app's log so we can see what went wrong. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

## Common questions

**How do I add a spot?** Tap + on the map, then New Spot. Add at least one photo, put the pin where the spot is and send it. Local editors check every spot before it goes on the map. Until then it shows as Pending in Contribute → Your Spots, and if notifications are on, you hear when they decide.

**Why was my spot declined or taken down?** Contribute → Your Spots shows the reason. If you think it's wrong, send the spot again with more detail, or write to us.

**Something's wrong with a spot.** Open the spot, tap More, then Report Spot. Pick what's wrong and add a note if it helps. An admin looks at every report.

**Someone is bothering you.** Open their profile, tap More, then Report or Block. Whoever you report isn't told who reported them.

**A photo of yours is on OpenSkateMap without your permission.** Write to {{contact}} with a link to the spot, and we'll take the photo down.

**How do I delete my account?** Profile → Delete Account. It deletes your account right away. Spots and photos you contributed stay, without your name. The [Privacy Policy](/privacy) says what's deleted.

**Notifications and analytics.** Profile → Notifications chooses which notifications you get. Profile → Share Analytics turns usage analytics off.
